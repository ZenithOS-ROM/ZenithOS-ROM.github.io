ZenithOS

ZenithOS is a lightweight operating system based on AOSP 11, designed for older devices such as the Pixel 4a and many others. It delivers a smooth, responsive experience while keeping the system clean, efficient, and easy to use.

About ZenithOS

Our goal is to provide the best possible user experience by focusing on stability, performance, and simplicity, ZenithOS is built to make older devices feel fast and enjoyable again, without unnecessary bloat or distractions

Whether you use your phone for everyday tasks, development, or simply want a lightweight Android experience, ZenithOS aims to deliver a reliable and polished system that you can enjoy
